## Original contributions

- A source-tier model tuned to Chinese-web failure modes such as repost chains and platform-metric overreach.
- A license-aware repository-to-Skill workflow that explicitly separates ideas, adaptations, and verbatim reuse.
- A transparent benchmark runner with blocking assertions and artifact checks.
- A shared standard-library-only CLI, negative tests, sample artifacts, and CI integration.
- Four product-lifecycle Skills covering opportunity discovery, traceable requirements, Agent UX, and claim-safe launches.
- Two original interactive prototypes for autonomy policy and AI feature tradeoffs, both useful without model access.
