# Narrative patterns

- Problem to outcome: best when pain and measurable change are clear.
- Before and after: best when the same task can be shown under comparable conditions.
- Proof walkthrough: best for unfamiliar or high-trust AI capabilities; show input, process, evidence, and result.
- Use-case montage: best when one capability serves several distinct segments; avoid implying all use cases are equally validated.

Open with the user consequence, not a feature inventory. Place proof close to the associated claim, and end with one concrete call to action.
