# Scoring

Score dimensions independently; do not reverse-engineer scores to justify a preferred idea.

- Evidence strength: source quality, freshness, independence, and directness.
- User value: frequency, severity, and importance of the job.
- Feasibility: access to data, model reliability, workflow integration, and operational burden.
- Strategic fit: distribution, differentiation, and ability to learn faster than alternatives.

Use `priority = evidence_strength × user_value × feasibility × strategic_fit`. The number is a sorting aid, not a forecast. A score below 3 for evidence strength must be labeled exploratory.
